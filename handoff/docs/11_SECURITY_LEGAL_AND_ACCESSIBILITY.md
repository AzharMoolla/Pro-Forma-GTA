# Security, licensing, legal risk and accessibility

## Security
Development projects may contain confidential land bids, seller identities, lender proposals, investor information, construction quotes and personal information. Default to minimal data collection, least-privilege roles, encryption, separate public-source and customer data domains, secure uploads, project access logs and tenant isolation. Allow full model export and deletion. Do not feed confidential deal data into AI by default.

## Legal / data rights
- Publicly downloadable **does not equal commercially redistributable**. Altus benchmarks are proprietary and need rights clearance before bundling full per-square-foot tables.
- Open government datasets each have their own attribution/license terms; respect specific municipality/authority requirements.
- Cite original statutes/bylaws and government material, never replace them with AI summarization as the legal source of truth.
- Model outputs are estimates, not tax advice, binding lender terms, municipal fee quotations, legal planning opinions or government program approvals.
- Keep project owner and terms governing eligible uploads/export; verify compliance for client material and datasets.

## Accessibility and usability
WCAG 2.2 AA as target: keyboard-only forms, clear focus, visible units, ARIA labels and statuses, predictable skip navigation, screen-reader-friendly data tables, high contrast, reduced motion, zoom/reflow, accessible chart table alternatives, field-level descriptions, error summaries and undo/redo. For advanced financial models, support efficient keyboard edits and CSV/XLSX export.

## Human control
AI may recommend but never auto-approve financial or regulatory facts. Every important override and calculated funding claim includes approver and timestamp. Offline/viewer exports are fully intelligible even if third-party feeds later go offline.
