# Executive brief — the product we are building

## Problem
Most small/midscale development feasibility starts in brittle spreadsheets: inaccessible cell logic, inconsistent space definitions, static policy/rate inputs, poor source traceability, manual scenario duplication, and attractive numbers that can hide modeling errors. Sophisticated enterprise suites exist but can be heavy and hard to navigate.

## Proposed positioning
A **GTA-first policy-aware development finance workspace** that scales from early land screenings to professional multi-phase underwriting. Differentiation is the **combination** of finance quality, explainable source-linked results, scenario reactivity, local policy/program knowledge and a calm, polished interface—not simply checkbox styling.

## Target users
Developer/sponsor, land-use planner, architect, development consultant, quantity surveyor, analyst, lender/pre-underwriting team, investment partner, government analyst, advanced student. Professional outputs must have permission/confidence gates; same core engine can expose tailored modes.

## Three coordinated engines
1. **Financial** — site acquisition, construction, phasing, revenue, ops, financing, debt sizing, sources/uses, returns.
2. **Policy** — municipal/regional/provincial/federal rules, charges, credits, deferrals, grants, rebates, CMHC financing screening, timing/geography.
3. **Construction** — sourced benchmarks, quantity-based estimates, escalation, area/cost reconciliation, contingencies; user/contractor/licensed data.

## User promise
"What will this project cost and earn, why does the result look this way, which assumptions are least certain, and what changes could make it viable?" Click on any KPI to reveal lineage and trace its sources.

## First market/coverage
City of Toronto and Oshawa/Durham; seed data-model abstractions for York, Peel, Halton and other GTA lower-tier municipalities without implying full validated coverage. Province-wide rules and federal programs can be modeled early when legally relevant.

## Guardrails
Policy monitoring can flag new rules but cannot grant eligibility. Any rate update shows before/after outcomes and must be accepted into a **new scenario version**. Retain reproducibility for historical approvals and investment committees.

## Success measures
Scenario reproducibility; model balance; tests passing; field provenance coverage; manual analyst time saved; comprehensible explanation of variance; verified source coverage; controlled update impact; exports professional users can audit.
