## Positioning hypothesis
A polished **policy-aware Canadian development modeling workspace** can differentiate on: GTA legislative/fee/program versioning, official source trails, actual effective-period cashflow, adjustable complexity, built-in formula quality checks and change-driven scenario diffs. This is a hypothesis, not validated product-market fit or pricing research.

## Research gaps
Competitive detailed feature/pricing comparison; interviews with 8–15 practitioners in consulting/development/lending; who can approve outputs; willingness to pay; municipal policy update effort and liability; cost-data licenses; XLSX import fidelity.

## Potential defensibility
Reliable versioned local-government data and an independently validated modeling core are harder to copy than a responsive UI. Building comprehensive municipal coverage entails a recurring operational commitment and legal-quality review process.
